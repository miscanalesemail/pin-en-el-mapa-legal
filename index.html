About Pin en el Mapa

Pin en el Mapa is an audiovisual content project dedicated to showcasing cities, provinces, and destinations from around the world through short, educational videos. Each video presents geographic, historical, and cultural facts about a specific place, accompanied by real images of the location.

Content is generated and published in an automated way, systematically covering thousands of destinations across six continents, with the goal of building an accessible audiovisual catalog of the planet's geographic and cultural diversity.
